# Heart

The Heart button means:

> "I like this."

## Behavior

- Hearts are unlimited.
- Hearting a Post adds it to the user's Love Potion.
- The Love Potion is a special heart/potion-themed collection on the user's Shelf.
- Hearting influences feed recommendations through connected discovery. Hearted Posts can make relevant Posts branching from or meaningfully connected to them eligible for recommendation.
- Heart is still not a command to recommend every connected Post. Connection strength, relevance, prior interactions, and other future recommendation signals can affect which connected content is recommended.
- Hearting does not change ownership of the Post.

## Love Potion

The Love Potion contains the user's Hearted items.

Opening it uses the normal collection-page layout but has a special blush effect:
- One blush circle at the exact center
- Two additional blush circles to the left and right, slightly lower than the center circle
- Blush lines around the circles
- After the transition, the normal Love Potion collection view appears

The exact animation can be refined during UI design.

## Meaning

Heart is the simple, lightweight appreciation action. It should require almost no thought from the user.
