# Heart

The Heart button means:

> "I like this."

## Behavior

- Hearts are unlimited.
- Hearting a post adds it to the user's personal Heart collection.
- The Heart collection appears as a special heart-shaped Tonic on the user's shelf.
- The Heart collection is a convenient way to revisit posts the user has liked.
- Hearting does not remove the post from the user's normal feed or otherwise change ownership of the post.

## Meaning

Heart is the simple, lightweight form of appreciation. It should require almost no thought from the user.
